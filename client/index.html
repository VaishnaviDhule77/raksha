Disaster-preparedness decision support · Prototype

Keep the community's food safe when storage fails.

RAKSHA helps coordinators model food inventories, storage resources and disaster scenarios — before disaster strikes — and turns the result into an offline emergency action plan.

Open dashboard Try the demo scenario

Demo Village · 100 households · flood + 24-hour power outage scenario included

01

Model the community

Record what food exists, how it is stored, and which refrigerators, freezers, cold boxes, generators and ice the community can rely on.

02

Simulate the failure

Apply flood, power-outage and heat conditions. RAKSHA evaluates configured safety rules, finds the capacity gap and optimises allocation of protected storage.

03

Prepare, even offline

Generate a printable emergency action plan, cache it on the device, and keep working from the offline plan when internet connectivity is lost.

RAKSHA — Resilient Adaptive Kitchen & Storage Hazard Assistant

Prototype decision-support tool. RAKSHA does not guarantee food safety, predict spoilage, or replace guidance from local authorities. It has not been tested in a real disaster.
